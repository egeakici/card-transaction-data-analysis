## Transformations and non-endorsement

Source files remain unchanged locally. Analytical derivatives apply documented parsing, normalisation, masking, label linkage and aggregation; these changes and the resulting interpretations are this project's work. Prepared Oklahoma exports were supplied after upstream preprocessing; the original download-stage transformations were not independently reproduced. See [the data card](outputs/DATA_CARD.md) and [decision ledger](outputs/DECISIONS.md).

No publisher, council, public body, repository host, author or institution is represented as sponsoring, approving, validating or endorsing this project or its interpretations. In particular, OGL attribution and the Solihull credit must not be presented as council approval. Logos are not licensed for promotional use by this project.
