# Ethics Statement

Status: 2026-11-04. No written publisher permission has been received. No institutional ethics approval, exemption or determination has been documented for this repository; no Bahçeşehir University committee decision, date or number is claimed.

> This study performs secondary descriptive analysis of retrospectively published administrative payment records and one academic bank-transaction dataset. It recruits no participants and performs no intervention or contact with data subjects. Public availability does not by itself establish that ethics review is unnecessary or that records contain no personal information. The supplied Oklahoma extracts already omit named cardholder columns; the pipeline excludes those columns if present and masks address-like purpose descriptions before persistence. Screening of published examples has limited coverage, including the absence of a source-name dictionary for the refreshed Oklahoma extracts, and does not certify complete anonymity. Raw records, private transaction-level intermediates and internal annotation samples are not released in this repository. Published source terms and unresolved rights questions are recorded in [DATA_LICENSING.md](DATA_LICENSING.md); no blanket compliance or redistribution-permission claim is made.

The responsible researchers must establish any required institutional review and applicable data-protection basis for their actual study and proposed release. If an ethics committee later determines that approval is not required, record the real institution, date and decision reference before adding that finding to a paper. Do not infer exemption from the absence of recruitment or from removal of some identifiers. Rights clearance and ethics/privacy assessment remain separate.
